Sources + methodology

Claims should be checkable.

This project separates primary-source facts, outside reporting, analysis and open questions.

Method

Primary records first.

Local factual claims are grounded in records released by the City of Granite Falls, executed contract material, government correspondence, public meeting records and Washington law. Outside reporting is used as context and is identified as such.

ScreenshotsMay be cropped for relevance and may redact personal contact details, signatures or financial account information.
ContextExcerpts should not be used where omitted surrounding material materially changes their meaning.
CapabilityA listed FlockOS feature is not described as actually used unless records support that conclusion.
Open questionsWhere the existing record does not establish an answer, the site labels the question rather than filling the gap with inference.
CorrectionsMaterial factual errors should be corrected when identified.
