Method

What the site treats as evidence.

Local claims are grounded primarily in City records, executed contract documents, invoices, permit material, Council records and Washington law. National legal and policy developments are linked to court dockets, official law text or established reporting.

An email establishes what the sender wrote; it does not automatically establish that the sender's legal interpretation was correct. A complaint establishes allegations; it does not establish liability. A report to an agency is not presented as an agency finding.

Screenshot excerpts are cropped for readability. Personal contact and financial information may be redacted. The underlying substantive wording is not altered inside the image.
