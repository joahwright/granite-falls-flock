About this project

Sources and methodology.

An independent Granite Falls public-information project built primarily from public records and primary legal sources.

Method

What the site treats as evidence.

Local claims are grounded primarily in City records, executed contract documents, invoices, permit material, Council records and Washington law. National legal and policy developments are linked to court dockets, official law text or established reporting.

An email establishes what the sender wrote; it does not automatically establish that the sender's legal interpretation was correct. A complaint establishes allegations; it does not establish liability. A report to an agency is not presented as an agency finding.

Screenshot excerpts are cropped for readability. Personal contact and financial information may be redacted. The underlying substantive wording is not altered inside the image.

Correction log

The September renewal deadline has been corrected, not silently removed.

Earlier versions of this site treated September 2026 as the non-renewal deadline. That interpretation came first from a September 10 meeting with City Manager Jeff Balentine in which the contract was reviewed around the October 17 execution date, and later from applying the MSA’s 30-day notice clause to the invoice’s Oct. 23–Oct. 22 service period.

Installment 6 brought the City’s February 4, 2026 Flock presentation back into view, where February 13 was identified as the renewal date. The MSA separately states that the term begins with first installation unless the Order Form says otherwise. After the discrepancy was raised, Balentine confirmed that the Terms and Conditions are correct. The site now uses February 13, 2027 as the working renewal date and mid-January 2027 as the working notice window.

This correction is a change in contract interpretation based on additional review. It is not presented as evidence that any City official intentionally provided inaccurate information.

Records methodology

City records and SCSO records are now tracked separately.

The City has stated that Installment 6 completed its production and has identified several operational categories as SCSO matters. Those categories are not treated as nonexistent merely because they were not in the City’s possession. The site now labels the open SCSO request separately and will distinguish later SCSO productions from the completed City production.

Direct answers from officials are treated as statements of the agency’s position. They are not substituted for underlying audit logs, configuration records or legal findings where those records remain outstanding.

DeFlock is the only outside organization this project describes as a collaborator or partner.
