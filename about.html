Sources

Public records first.

Local reporting on this site is based primarily on City records, executed contract documents, invoices, permit material, Council records, SCSO responses and Washington law. National developments link to court records, official law text or established reporting.

Screenshots are cropped for readability. Personal contact and financial information may be redacted; the substantive wording in the source image is not rewritten.

Correction log

Renewal timeline.

Earlier versions of the site used a September 2026 non-renewal deadline after focusing on the October 17 execution date and the invoice’s Oct. 23–Oct. 22 billing period.

Further review showed that the MSA and Order Form tie the 12-month term to first installation and validation unless the Order Form states otherwise. City records show installation began Feb. 13, 2025, and later City materials identify Feb. 13 as the renewal date. After I raised the discrepancy, City Manager Jeff Balentine confirmed that the MSA Terms and Conditions are correct.

The site now uses Feb. 13, 2027 as the working renewal date and mid-January 2027 as the working non-renewal notice window. The invoice’s Oct. 23–Oct. 22 period remains documented as a separate billing-period entry.

Records status
