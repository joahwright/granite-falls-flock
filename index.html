01 / Campaign

What has happened so far.

The campaign is built around the public record rather than speculation. Work to date includes a broad City public-records request, repeated review of City Council materials, public comments, meeting attendance, public outreach, and follow-up requests aimed at the parts of the system the original record did not reveal.

Records
PRA2026-21

Contracts, invoices, deployment material, correspondence and internal Flock discussions were obtained from the City.

Council
Public comment and meeting attendance

Flock has been raised directly in public meetings while contract review and ESSB 6002 compliance have moved through Council.

Public
Website, flyers and community outreach

The goal is to make the City's own record understandable without requiring residents to read hundreds of pages of production files.

Next
Audit logs, post-May correspondence and school records

New requests target actual system use, data sharing, retention, and when Granite Falls School District learned about the camera near the high school.

03 / The standard

Show the record.

This rebuild uses screenshots of the relevant portions of public records rather than forcing readers into entire PDFs. Screenshots are cropped for relevance and may redact personal contact or financial information, but substantive text is not rewritten inside the source image.

Fact. Source. Context. Then analysis.

Full underlying records should still be retained offline so any disputed excerpt can be checked against its source.
