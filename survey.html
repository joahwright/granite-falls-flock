Granite Falls community input

Take the survey. Sign if you agree.

Granite Falls residents are invited to share their views on the City's use of automated license plate readers. The survey is open to supporters, opponents and undecided residents. The petition is optional and is counted separately from survey responses.

How it works

Survey first. Petition second.

The first portion measures community opinion. You do not have to support this campaign or sign the petition to complete the survey.

Residents who support ending Granite Falls' Flock Safety ALPR program can then choose to add their name to the petition presented at the end.

Responses are counted separately

Survey responses will be analyzed as community feedback. Petition signatures will be counted separately and may be presented to the Granite Falls City Council.
