Location #07

The school camera.

The camera near Granite Falls High School now has a clearer factual record, but the statutory field-of-capture question remains unresolved by any court or Attorney General finding.

Current status

What is known — and what is still being investigated.

  • SCSO says the Penny Avenue/100th Street camera was never disabled or physically moved after ESSB 6002 took effect.
  • SCSO says the camera was re-aimed slightly north around May 1, 2026, and that the change intentionally reduced some data collection.
  • Chief Tom Dalton stated that City and SCSO counsel believe the placement complies with the statute and that the camera does not collect known data from the nearby school or place of worship.
  • Those statements describe the agencies’ position; they are not a court ruling or Attorney General finding.
  • The Granite Falls School District records request and the separate Attorney General concern remain relevant to the factual record.
Washington law

Schools are expressly protected.

Washington's 2026 ALPR law prohibits an agency from collecting ALPR data on the premises, immediate surroundings, or access to or from elementary and secondary schools. The statute also imposes a general 21-day retention ceiling, subject to enumerated exceptions.

Post-law change

The camera was re-aimed, not removed.

In his September 2026 response, Chief Dalton said the camera was re-aimed slightly more to the north around May 1 after concerns about public perception. He said SCSO knew the change would reduce some data collection and described the adjustment as an effort to acknowledge public concern and comply with the intent of SB 6002.

The response does not identify the exact lane, turn movement, entrance, exit or other traffic that stopped being captured. That distinction matters because the statute regulates where ALPR data is collected, not simply the physical pole location.

Pre-law use example

The January 2026 AED investigation shows how the camera was used before ESSB 6002 took effect.

Chief Dalton reported that the school district supplied video of a suspect vehicle after an AED was stolen from the Granite Falls High School sports fields. Deputies then entered the vehicle information and a defined time range into Flock and identified the suspect vehicle. Dalton described that result as significant to a broader regional theft investigation.

The incident occurred in January 2026, before ESSB 6002 took effect on March 30. It therefore documents a pre-law investigative use; it does not resolve whether the current camera placement and current searches comply with the later restricted-location rules.
