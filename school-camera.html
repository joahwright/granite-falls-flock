100th Street / Granite Falls High School

The school camera question.

The key factual questions are where the camera collects, when the School District learned about it, and what changed after Washington restricted ALPR collection around schools.

01 / What is documented

A camera on 100th Street.

City correspondence identifies a Flock camera on 100th Street and documents maintenance intended to keep its view unobstructed. City planning records also identify a high-school entrance along 100th Street.

This photograph proves the camera’s location and physical orientation only to the extent visible in the image. It does not establish the exact electronic field of capture.

02 / What Washington law says

Schools are specifically protected.

ESSB 6002 makes it unlawful for an agency to collect ALPR data on the premises, immediate surroundings, or access to or from elementary and secondary schools, subject to the statute’s definitions and scope.
