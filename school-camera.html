Location #07

The school camera.

The camera near Granite Falls High School presents a concrete compliance question under Washington’s 2026 ALPR statute.

Current status

What is known — and what is still being investigated.

  • Concern raised with the Washington Attorney General's Office.
  • Public-records request submitted to Granite Falls School District.
  • Records sought include notice to the District, communications with the City and Sheriff's Office, field-of-view documentation, privacy concerns and ESSB 6002 compliance discussions.
  • No conclusion should be drawn about the District's prior knowledge until those records are produced.
Washington law

Schools are expressly protected.

Washington's 2026 ALPR law prohibits an agency from collecting ALPR data on the premises, immediate surroundings, or access to or from elementary and secondary schools. The statute also imposes a general 21-day retention ceiling, subject to enumerated exceptions.
