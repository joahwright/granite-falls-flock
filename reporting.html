Reporting + research

Flock in Washington and beyond.

Local reporting, Washington research, legislative coverage and national stories that help place Granite Falls’ contract in context.

Why these stories are here

Local decisions do not happen in isolation.

Events in other jurisdictions do not establish what happened in Granite Falls. They do show how Flock’s network, public-records obligations, access controls, state law and documented misuse have been tested elsewhere.
