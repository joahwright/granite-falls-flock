Legal + policy developments

A live national legal dispute.

Washington law, federal litigation and rapidly changing state and local policy now frame the Granite Falls decision.

Federal litigation

Fourth Amendment litigation cuts both ways.

Jan. 27, 2026

Schmidt v. City of Norfolk

Plaintiffs challenged Norfolk's widespread ALPR deployment. After the case survived an earlier dismissal effort, the district court granted summary judgment to Norfolk in January 2026 and upheld that deployment against the Fourth Amendment challenge. That adverse precedent belongs in any fair legal summary.

U.S. District Court · E.D. Virginia

Aug. 25, 2026

Schulte v. Flock Group Inc.

A proposed class action was filed in the Northern District of Georgia against Flock Group. The complaint alleges misuse of location information for personal surveillance and stalking. These are allegations, not adjudicated facts.

Washington

ESSB 6002 materially changed the rules.

Washington enacted new limits including a general 21-day retention ceiling, prohibited-location rules covering schools and other protected places, sharing restrictions, registration/certification requirements and audit/reporting obligations.
