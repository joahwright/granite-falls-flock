Current objective

Seek termination at the earliest legal point.

After Council allowed the automatic-renewal process to proceed, this project's stated objective remains ending the Flock agreement as soon as the contract and applicable law permit.

Contract status

Renewal does not eliminate every exit path.

The executed MSA does not contain a general termination-for-convenience clause. It does identify several routes that can end the agreement before a future anniversary, depending on the facts and legal basis.

Non-renewal, if the window remains open

Section 7.1 requires at least 30 days' notice before the end of the current term. The original invoice's Oct. 23–Oct. 22 service period creates an unresolved question about whether an apparent Sept. 22 deadline still controls.

Material breach

Section 7.2 allows the non-breaching party to terminate after written notice if a material breach is not cured within 30 days.

Non-appropriation

Section 11.15 provides a 30-day termination right without penalty or other cost where the public-funds non-appropriation provision applies.

Negotiated exit

The parties may agree in writing to modify the contract, creating a potential path to a mutually agreed termination even if another unilateral exit provision does not apply.
