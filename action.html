Decision point

Require a public decision.

The question is whether Granite Falls should continue operating a privately hosted network of automated license plate readers. Renewal should be debated in public with the current contract, current law and actual local record in front of the Council.

Campaign position

End Granite Falls’ use of Flock Safety automated license plate readers rather than renewing or expanding the system.
