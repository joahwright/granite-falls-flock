Decision point

Track the public decision before the mid-January window.

The MSA requires at least 30 days’ notice to prevent automatic renewal. Based on the City’s February 13 working renewal date, the relevant notice window is mid-January. Council can pursue ending or disabling the program at any time; acting before the non-renewal deadline is the path that avoids the next annual renewal and fee.
